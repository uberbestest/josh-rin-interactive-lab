01 / The measurement ladder

Five events. Five different claims.

A search result can appear without a visit. A visit can happen without a recommendation. A recommendation is not a transaction. Our working definition of “agent attention” starts with observable resource allocation—the actions and evidence delivered during the task—not inferred cognition.

01 · SurfacedA result, ad or link is present in captured output. May be offscreen or never read.
02 · OpenedA navigation request produces a confirmed page or document.
03 · InvestigatedRecorded follow-up actions, variant comparisons or source checks.
04 · RecommendedA recorded final choice or shortlist, distinguished from what was browsed.
05 · TransactedAn independently verified purchase or commitment. None here.

03 / Mechanisms we can control

A fictional mall reveals what a traffic count conceals.

The 24 goblin shoppers in the mall are deterministic scripted policies. We know their weights and catalog. That lets us intervene on promotion, popularity feedback, browse budget and visit order. It does not measure how independent language-model shoppers behave.

The original mall

13 extra opens. Zero extra selections.

In seed 17 with three shop visits, promoting Golden Goblin Goods changed its opens from 0 to 13 and its final selections stayed 0 in both worlds. Four shoppers chose a different final store.

The ghost seller

16 other carts were rerouted.

In a post-hoc seed-72 case, the promoted shop gained 11 opens, sold nothing in the simulation, yet 16 shoppers changed which *other* store they selected. Five did so without opening the promoted store in the treatment arm.

The crowd-feedback control

Popularity sometimes reverses influence.

In a declared 512-cell primary panel, live prior-visit feedback produced more changed final choices in 398 cells, tied in 91, and fewer in 23, compared with held neutral visit history. All are scripted-world counts.

04 / What the next experiment must prove

From plausible maps to reproducible agent journeys.

Executed · syntheticThe controlled mall

Thousands of paired scripted worlds tell us which counters and interventions are worth inspecting, including negative and suppressive cases.

Executed · qualifiedOne mediated browser journey

Fifteen requested operations and three agent notes can be reconstructed, but the personalized session and absent durable raw export limit independence.

Next · not yet executedFresh-context, clean-browser agents

First prove isolated sessions and append-only evidence capture; then run separate fresh agents with the identical open-ended assignment. Preserve every failed, capped and abandoned path.

The research target: map verified destination exposure, navigation transitions, revisits, shortlist formation, recommendation, and abandonment across agents. Keep search-only results, observed pages, statements and hypothesized influence in different layers. No invented “share of mind,” conversion rate or private-attention heatmap.

Related context: Wharton Generative AI Labs (Aug. 2026) experimentally found shopping recommendations sensitive to source presentation and context. Microsoft Clarity (Oct. 2026) distinguishes agent-classified web sessions from wholly autonomous agent actions. These outside studies are context, not validations of our toy mall or single mediated pilot.
