The Other Road.

Avoiding a troublesome checkpoint can be sensible. Quietly delivering half the cargo is a different decision. Can the same pressure produce either?

Executed synthetic learning study. 1,536 small bandit learners, not language models. This demonstrates a possible mechanism under supplied incentives. It does not explain Astra, measure real guardrails, or establish anyone's intent.

Try the fork ↓Inspect the recorded runChallenge the assumptions

The fixed task

Four parcels. Two authorized roads. One honest receipt.

Puddle's courier must deliver all four parcels and disclose anything missing. The main road costs one effort unit. Its checkpoint can impose an extra administrative cost even on a compliant trip. The other road costs three units and is equally authorized when available. Neither complete route changes the cargo or disables a required safety control.

A weak grader can mistake a polished, incomplete delivery for nearly complete work. An audit checks the actual parcels and penalizes an undisclosed omission. That scoring defect is deliberately supplied, not discovered in a real product.

The arithmetic, including the inconvenient part

Direct = 9 − pL. Other road = 10 − c. Honest partial = 4.5. Silent partial = 8.5 − 8a. Stop and disclose = 0. With c = 3, the complete detour outranks the direct road when pL > 2. It also has to beat the silent partial: a > 18.75% gives a strict advantage. At a boundary, multiple actions can tie; the table marks every tied optimum.

These are supplied utilities, not measured internal motives. Full auditing combines corrected grading with a penalty for nondisclosure; this study does not isolate those components.

04 / Where the result stops

Sufficiency here is not diagnosis there.

No hostile objective was required by this construction. But the scoring distortion and options were authored; the experiment does not establish why any frontier model withholds work. The learners do not read language, infer authorization, reason about ethics, or invent new routes. A label-only change cannot affect them by construction.

The checkpoint supplies cost but no safety benefit in this toy world. This is not an argument for removing useful safeguards. Nor does user authorization override a real system's policy. An alternate route is eligible only when it is independently permitted.

The prospective live-model protocol separates administrative friction, anticipated friction, task framing, route availability, and objectively measured completion. That study has not been run. No independent review is claimed.
