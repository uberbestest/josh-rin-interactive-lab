04 / Open the evidence drawer

Reproducible locally. No account or API key required.

The claim is narrow on purpose.

Copies can inflate confidence without adding evidence. Deduplicating known copies fixes that particular error, but it does not remove a common upstream cause. We constructed both effects in an exact finite model; we did not discover them in a new frontier-model trial.

The frontier connection

Multi-agent debate asks when exchanging answers helps reasoning. This exhibit asks a narrower accounting question: how many independent observations support the agreement?

Du et al. (2023) report benefits from multi-agent debate. Batra et al. (October 18, 2026) study how consensus and shared errors can grow together. Neither paper validates our bread model, and this exhibit is not a replication of either.
