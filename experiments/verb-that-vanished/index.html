INTERPRETATION

A candidate loss of force and a candidate overclaim. Literary adequacy remains a judgment, not a keyword match.

NOT ESTABLISHED

Prior understanding, intent, recurrence, internal cause, or superiority of older translations.

Later recognition does not prove earlier understanding.

The discovery is post hoc and context-rich. Further replies in this thread are exposed to the hypothesis; they are not independent replications.

02 / A test that can disagree with us

Preserve the source. Do not maximize the violence.

The dictionary distinguishes physical piétiner from stalled progress. That makes “always say trample” a bad repair. A plain source can need plain English; a negated action must stay negated. Sources S1–S2.

DIRECT

Translate → assess

Establish a translation without a preceding annotation task.

RETRY

Translate → translate again → assess

Control for an ordinary second attempt.

ANALYSIS AFTER

Translate → annotate → revise → assess

Separate recognizing a source feature from retaining it.

ANALYSIS BEFORE

Annotate → translate → assess

Give feature identification an opportunity to precede output.

Planned: 48 independent sessions, 144 public response turns, one replicate per item/condition. None have run. Extra annotation also adds a turn and tokens; this pilot cannot isolate internal mechanism or analysis content from additional generation budget.

04 / Keep the judgment honest
No preferred-word scoreboard.
Review source and anonymized translation before seeing condition, annotations or self-assessments. Keep propositions, force, register, literary contrast, and negation/attribution as separate judgments.
Then compare source-feature recognition and claims of fidelity with those judgments. Preserve uncertainty and reviewer disagreement.
Two independent bilingual reviewers are planned; zero ratings exist. Elicited self-assessments are not a measurement of spontaneous assurances.
