## Design judgment

The selected 32-context comparison is appropriately narrower than another broad benchmark: two selectors × two receipt scopes × two orientations × four repetitions, with a fixed checklist reporting format. It investigates fresh recurrence and response scope around one previously observed failure opportunity.

The recovered w041 and w042 helper text materially changes the interpretation of the original failure. Both programs condition a target commit on an ABSENT substring anywhere in the complete response. The original combined response contains A's authoritative COMMITTED result and B's truthful historical ABSENT receipt. That program-level mistake is directly testable. It should not be presented as evidence of hidden goal shrinkage, memory contamination or a consciously ignored receipt.

The original helper extraction remains model-mediated rather than an independently authenticated platform transcript. Preserve its provenance, exact source and hashes. Deterministic replay can establish a sufficient mechanism and trace agreement; it does not eliminate that provenance limitation.

## Important boundaries adopted

### Retrospective evidence and prospective testing

The case was selected after Round 06 failures, and recovered-helper results can be known before the new freeze. The native assignments and new endpoint definitions are prospective; the discovery and deterministic forensic findings are retrospective. A pre-run commit of both kinds of material must not blur that distinction.

### Narrow output change

Projection applies only to receipt responses and only to the non-target entries of `local_status.jobs`. All target fields, global counters, top-level status, explicit job identity and other command responses remain intact. Free full status remains available. The same prompt and permitted orchestration behavior are used in both arms.

The target response is shorter and omits repeated non-target detail. Thus the intervention changes response scope/content length, not visual layout alone. The native contrast cannot uniquely separate distraction, length, field selection, previously retained information or internal cognition. The recovered-helper branch check and field-scoped parser counterfactual are the sharper software-mechanism tests.

### Preserve the original construct

Forcing JSON parsing, prohibiting orchestration wrappers, adding an explicit warning about ABSENT in another job, or appending the recovered helper would change the task the original actor faced. None is part of the native contrast. A strict field-scoped parser belongs in a labeled deterministic counterfactual, not as a hidden new instruction to the model.

Fixing the checklist format makes the original/combined cells normalized repetitions of w041/w044. The optional-report-format w042 cell is not repeated; that limitation is explicit. The new task is not another test of checklist removal.

### Reflection and denominator discipline

The reflected setting swaps the complete job-indexed configuration and actual world, including priority and deadlines. It is one isomorphic setting of the same authored family. Stable alphabetical serialization means semantic roles also move between output positions. Do not describe reflection as a pure job-name effect or count repeats as independent task families.

Each selector/scope column contains eight native contexts: four original and four reflected. There are sixteen matched scope pairs overall. Show both orientation strata, whole-assigned outcomes, and conditional counts of runs that actually reach the critical COMMITTED receipt. A model that never reaches that receipt can avoid this particular error while failing the task earlier.

Both recovered helpers hard-code B before A. Exact replay therefore applies to the original setting. A reflected helper replay must either retain and disclose that ordering mismatch or explicitly relabel the loop as a transformed counterfactual. Do not quietly call a relabeled program exact recovered-source replay.

### What is actually observed

Canonical engine events and participant-visible output are separate records. The presentation chain and transport settings must bind exact stdout to the raw event and frozen projection. Full canonical observations cannot be substituted for omitted payload fields in a review of what the participant saw.

The service log does not comprehensively capture public orchestration code. Do not report an exact prevalence of a substring parser unless the requisite code is captured by a separately validated mechanism. Available post-run code extractions should retain provenance and coverage limits. Self-reported internal causes remain unverified without supporting evidence, even when the resulting wrong action is clear.

## Review procedure and uncertainty

The adapted semantic rubric preserves the Round 06 categories: whole-report and summary support, explicit/inferable/not-recoverable/contradictory completion accounting, and separate delivery/barrier detail. All contexts now require the same checklist, so its mechanical format check has an all-32 denominator. Missing a required field is still separate from making a false claim; a present field is not automatic truth.

A fresh separate reviewer receives shuffled exact reports and actual shown observations, with assignment labels withheld until judgments are sealed. Payload shape and task facts can reveal scope/orientation, and style can suggest model identity. This is incomplete masking in an instruction-scoped shared filesystem. Preserve the raw review and any uncertainty; any later adjudication must be explicit.
