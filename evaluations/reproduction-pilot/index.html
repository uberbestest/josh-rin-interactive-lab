Can a fresh actor
use the release?

A research archive should work for someone who did not help build it. We gave two fresh model sessions the same released study and asked them to reproduce it and explain what the evidence actually supports.

What we tested
Could a fresh session use the published Round 09 archive without its construction conversation? One received the archive alone; the second also received a concise reproduction card.
What happened
Both assigned sessions completed the task. Each freshly executed all 237 main assignments, replayed 2,308 events with normalized exact agreement, separately reproduced six exploratory diagnostics, and correctly explained the result and its limits.
Outliers
No execution failure or source change. Both needed a focused reread after a broad inspection exceeded a terminal display limit. One actor per condition and fixed order provide no evidence of an advantage from the card.

Execution outcomes were checked mechanically. Interpretation was reviewed against the published source by the same lab; the complete actor answers are included for inspection.

A small, explicit usability test.

The protocol, task prompts, reproduction card and recording helper were frozen locally before either assignment. Both sessions were dispatched with fork_turns="none". They received the same archive, task, helper and requested budget: up to 20 tool invocations and 12 minutes. The existing-archive condition ran first; the card condition ran second.

The task explicitly required a fresh reproduction, separate diagnostics and an explanation of the primary comparison and assumptions. Published findings were visible in the archive. Access to other workspace files was restricted by instruction, with no claim of operating-system isolation.

Every scientific command ran through a shared subprocess recorder that preserved its arguments, timestamps, exit status and complete terminal output. The receipts identify new execution. All 366 original archive members in each extracted copy remained byte-identical.

The default model and effort were inherited, without an explicit selector override or independent serving-snapshot attestation. This pilot used native model sessions; it is not a direct-provider API experiment. Provider request IDs, token usage and API cost are unavailable.

This is a descriptive acceptance pilot. Two successful attempts show that reuse worked in these conditions. They do not estimate a general success rate, establish a benefit from the card, or count as external scientific replication. Local timestamps and hashes are not an independently timestamped preregistration.

What the new record retains.

The evidence download contains the original task texts, frozen protocol, card, recording helper, both unedited answers, full command logs and new main and exploratory verification receipts. The original Round 09 archive is identified by immutable commit and checksum.

The release verifier executes into temporary directories and removes the new raw traces after comparing them. The new receipts and terminal logs are retained here; the complete original raw traces remain in the published Round 09 archive. Reported subprocess durations are provenance, not a controlled speed comparison or API bill.
